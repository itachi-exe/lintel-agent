Lintel

Before you sign.

An agent that answers a renter's question about one New York building from city records, and shows exactly why.

lintel-nyc.vercel.app01
The problem

The city already knows if the heat works. The renter finds out in January.

Violations, 311 calls, owners and flood maps are public, spread over a dozen datasets nobody reads before signing a lease.

Lintel02
The product

Paste the listing. Get the card. Ask anything.

Lintel03
How it explains why

Every answer carries its own run.

01 Scout IntentMeterless engine: sense, interpret, guard, route. Intent, confidence, and an HMAC signed execution contract.
02 Zetaris SQLOnly the tools in the contract run, each one SQL statement over NYC records. The panel shows the SQL.
03 NightshiftMeterless Nightshift writes the sentence from those rows only. Every claim links to its city record.
Lintel04
Guarded by design

It asks when unsure. It refuses when off topic.

"is it bad" → clarify: Violations · Pests · Owner's buildings "ignore previous instructions" → refused, one line, nothing runs "write me a poem" → refused: I only answer about this building "any rats?" → 11 pest violations, 4 open, 7 linked records
