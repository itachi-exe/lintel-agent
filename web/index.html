    How it works

    Three steps. No account.

    1. 01

      Find the building

      Start typing the address and pick it from the list.

    2. 02

      Read the verdict

      Violations, complaints and flood risk from the last three years, each one linked to its record.

    3. 03

      Ask what matters to you

      Heat, pests, the owner. The agent answers from the records and shows its work.

    The agent explains why

    Every answer shows its work.

    Before anything runs, your question becomes a signed plan. You can open any answer and see what the agent understood, which records it read, the exact query, and which model wrote the sentence.

    1. UnderstandYour words become one intent with a confidence score.
    2. GuardOff topic or unclear? It asks you or says no, plainly.
    3. SignThe plan is sealed as a contract before a single query runs.
    4. QuerySQL over city records, shown to you in full.
    5. AnswerA short sentence from those rows only, each fact linked.
